Nexus Transfer Manager - Fragment Record v3

Record byte order: network / big-endian

Offset  Size  Field
0x00    4     magic = NXFR
0x04    4     session_id (uint32)
0x08    2     sequence (uint16, zero based)
0x0A    2     total_fragments (uint16)
0x0C    4     payload_length (uint32)
0x10    4     payload_crc32 (uint32)
0x14    ...   payload bytes

The scheduler catalog stores SHA-256 over PAYLOAD BYTES ONLY (not the NXFR header).
Repeated records are allowed after an uplink retry. A retry is authoritative only if its payload hash matches the scheduler catalog.
